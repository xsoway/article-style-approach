Article Style Approach

个人文章写作与去 AI 味的思路复现——把「写作手感」工程化成可复现的检查。

A reproducible methodology for writing in your own voice and systematically removing the AI flavor.

机制与数据分离 · 证据优先 · 分级去 AI 味 + 硬门禁
Mechanism/data separation · evidence-first · tiered de-AI + hard tone gate
GitHub ↗ README.md 中文 README

01这是什么What it is

一份方法/思路仓库，不是代码实现。它把「用个人风格写中文文章、并系统性去 AI 味」讲成一套可以自己复现的方法论，并讲清真实落地时用到的数据结构、评分逻辑与检查流程。

A methodology-only repo, not an implementation. It turns "write in your own voice and systematically de-AI" into a reproducible approach, and explains the data structures, scoring logic, and check flow used in practice.

✓ 机制与数据分离

流程不硬编码任何「谁」的词表或禁用表达；换一套数据即换一种风格，私人语料永远留在本地。

The flow never hard-codes anyone's vocabulary or banned expressions; swap data to change style, private corpus stays local.

✓ 证据优先

文章里的人、事、数字只能来自你本次提供的可核验素材，不补造经历、数字、对话。

People, facts, and numbers in an article come only from your verifiable input — no fabricated experiences, numbers, or dialogue.

✓ 分级去 AI 味（L1–L6）

把「像不像 AI」拆成六级、六维、0–100 分，改稿有对象、可复现。

Break AI-likeness into six levels, six dimensions, 0–100 score — something concrete and reproducible to edit.

✓ 一软一硬

去 AI 味是提示，语气门禁是唯一硬性发布检查；人始终在回路里。

De-AI is a suggestion, the tone gate is the only hard publish check; a human stays in the loop.

02为什么需要Why

大多数人卡在「写出来一股 AI 味」和「想改但不知道查什么」上。这套思路把问题拆成三件可分别解决的事。

Most people get stuck on "output reads AI-flavored" and "don't know what to check." This approach splits the problem into three separately solvable pieces.

Instinct（反例）

「写出来一股 AI 味」/ 凭感觉改完还是像 / 干脆放弃

"Reads AI-flavored" / fix by feel, still AI-like / give up

This approach（本方案）

机制与数据分离 / 证据优先 / 分级定位 + 硬门禁

mechanism/data separation / evidence-first / tiered locating + hard gate

03三步闭环Three-step loop

整个流程是「准备 → 写稿 → 检查」的闭环，不是写一稿就完事。

The whole flow is a "prepare → write → check" loop, not "finish in one draft."

prepare

准备

Prepare

读你的语料 + 词表，生成风格候选报告（词/句式/连接词命中）。只读不改。

Read your corpus + vocabulary, generate a style candidate report (words/phrases/connectors). Read-only.

write

写稿

Write

整理本次可核验事实 → 读风格报告 → 用你的判断照着事实写。

Compile verifiable facts → read the style report → write from facts with your judgment.

review

检查

Review

分级定位 AI 痕迹（提示）+ 语气硬门禁（拦截）；检查不改正文。

Tiered de-AI locating (suggestion) + hard tone gate (block); checks never rewrite.
